## 11. 補聴器の信号処理とその限界（2026-08-04 ゼミ宿題② で追加。関連研究1段落の出典）

| 文献 | 使いどころ | 押さえた事実 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Chung 2004, Trends in Amplification 8(3):83-124](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4111442/) | 補聴器＝帯域分割＋多チャネル処理という基本。指向性の原理的副作用 | "less sensitive to speech or environmental sounds coming from the back hemisphere"／前方以外から聞きたい音が来る場面が約20%(Walden et al. 2003の孫引き) |
| [NICE Guideline NG98 (2018) 補聴器マイク・雑音抑制の evidence review](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK536536/) | 「雑音抑制は了解度でなく快適性」の公的根拠 | "the speech-to-noise ratio remains unchanged, but there is the potential to improve listener comfort"／"may come from a location where the microphone is least sensitive (for example, from behind)" |

| [Brimijoin et al. 2014, Ear Hear 35(5):e204-e212](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4232295/) | **最も効く実測**。指向性は後方への振り向きを悪化させる | "increasing to nearly half the trials at target angles of ±150°"（初動が逆方向） |
